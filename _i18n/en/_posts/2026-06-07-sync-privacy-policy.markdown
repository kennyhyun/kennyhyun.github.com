**Last updated: June 2026**

## Summary

yeoyou sync does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party.

---

## What we collect

**Nothing.** yeoyou sync has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no remote servers operated by us.

All data stays on your device and your own home server.

## What the app accesses

| Permission | Purpose | Sent to |
|---|---|---|
| Photos & Videos | Read files to back up | Your home server only |
| Wi-Fi state | Check connection before syncing | Never transmitted |
| Internet | Transfer files to your server | Your home server only |

Your files are sent directly from your phone to the server you configured. We never see them.

## Data stored on your device

- Server connection info (IP address, alias) — stored locally in the app database
- List of already-synced files — stored locally to avoid re-uploading

This data never leaves your device except to your own server.

## Third-party services

None. yeoyou sync does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising libraries.

## Children's privacy

yeoyou sync does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
